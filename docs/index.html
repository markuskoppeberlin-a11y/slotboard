10 clipboard slots, zero clutter.

Copy and paste the way you always do. Add a digit and you have ten of them.

12345 67890
⌘C+1–0stores the selection in a slot
⌘V+1–0pastes it anywhere
Download for Mac Windows Linux

Free and open source. Ctrl instead of ⌘ on Windows and Linux.

Nothing new to learn

No window, no history, no pop-ups. Without a digit, ⌘C and ⌘V behave exactly as before.

Everything fits in a slot

Plain and formatted text, images, files and app content like Figma layers.

Your clipboard stays yours

After pasting from a slot, the normal clipboard is put back.

Private by design

Works offline, records no keystrokes, ignores passwords, forgets everything when you quit.
